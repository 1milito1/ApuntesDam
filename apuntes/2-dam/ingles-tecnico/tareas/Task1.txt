Imagine you've just joined a tech company and you're collaborating with your classmates as if they were your colleagues. This forum is your space to introduce yourself, discuss your role, and collaborate on a project!

Instructions:

In order to do this task, first look at the notes on the platform. Then, you are expected to make at least 3 interventions:

1. First post: self-introduction. 👤Introduce yourself as if you had just joined a tech company.

Use what you have already learned during the course to present yourself to your colleagues
State your name and role (e.g.: software developer, UI/UX designer, project manager).
Share a brief background (experience, studies, interests related to tech).
Mention your strengths and what you can contribute to the team.
End with a question to encourage replies and show interest for your workmates (e.g.: What's your area of expertise?).
Example: Hello everyone. My name is Anne and I have just joined this company as a junior mobile developer. I have recently completed my degree in Computer science, and I am passionate about building Android applications. My strengths are debugging and UI design. I'm excited to collaborate with you all! What programming languages do you use the most?

2. Second post: reply a teammate and discuss a work task. 💬Read one of your classmates' introductions and reply as a colleague. 

Acknowledge their post and highlight a shared interest or skill.
Suggest a collaboration (e.g.: We could work together on a project).
Ask a follow-up question to keep the conversation going.
Example: Hi Lucas! Nice to meet you. I also enjoy UI design, especially for iOS apps. Maybe we could compare design trends for both operating systems? Do you have experience with Figma or Sketch?

3. Third post: discuss a common work challenge. 🔍 Imagine your team is facing a challenge at work, and share your approach to solving it.

Describe a common issue in the IT industry (e.g.: time management, miscommunication, problems with debugging).
Suggest a possible solution.
Ask for input from others on how they would handle the situation.
Example: Our team is working on a project, and we’re struggling with version control. Some teammates keep overwriting each other’s work. I think we should use Git more effectively and maybe set clear commit guidelines. Have you faced this issue before? How do you manage it?

💡TIPS:

Be clear, professional and respectful
Respond to different classmates
Try to imagine you are in this situation in order to get hands-on practice on a real-world setting.
